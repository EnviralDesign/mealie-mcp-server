# Canonical Food Policy

## Create Or Link Structured Food

Use structured food when the phrase identifies a reusable grocery object:

- produce: `cucumber`, `bell pepper`, `lime`, `fresh basil`
- dairy: `2% milk`, `mozzarella cheese sticks`
- beverages: `almond milk`, `whole bean coffee`, `beer`
- frozen: `frozen pizza`
- pantry: `cereal`

Add metadata for new foods:

- plural name,
- concise grocery-oriented description,
- label,
- aliases for genuine alternate names or common wording.

## Keep As Note-Only

Use note-only when the shopper needs freedom or the phrase is too broad:

- `Bread`
- `Fresh fruits`
- `Box of salad`
- `Sliced turkey or chicken`
- `snacks for school`
- `something easy for Friday dinner`

Attach a label if useful, but do not pollute the food database.

## Use Food Plus Note

When the canonical item is reusable but the request has a brand, quality, or choice constraint, link the food and keep the constraint as `note`.

Examples:

- food `beer`, note `Leinenkugel's for today`
- food `cereal`, note `not 100% garbage (contains fiber and/or protein)`
- food `bell pepper`, note `orange`
- food `vegetable oil`, note `(cooking oil)`
- food `whole bean coffee`, no note needed

## Labels

Use labels for shopping behavior:

- bakery
- baking & cooking
- beans & grains
- beverages
- condiments
- dairy
- frozen
- household
- meat & seafood
- pasta & noodles
- produce
- snacks
